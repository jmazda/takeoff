Take-Off

Live one economic reformer's 25-year career in a real country, with real data. Your choices matter, but less than you think. Most careers don't produce a take-off.

Loading country data…

How it works

  • You start mid-career in a real low- or middle-income country, drawn at random, with its real starting conditions.
  • Each year you pick one focus and spend political capital and budget. Shocks roll at real base rates. Reshuffles happen when they happen.
  • Four real sibling countries that started at your income run alongside you. Most of them won't take off either.
  • At the end, the game reruns your career to tell you how much was you and how much was luck.
Data: World Bank WDI, Enterprise Surveys and Worldwide Governance Indicators. Every judgement call is listed on the methods page. No login, nothing leaves your browser.
