How it works

  1. You are appointed mid-career in a real low- or middle-income country, drawn at random, with its real starting hand: income, exports, power, ports, and what firms say blocks them.
  2. Each year you pick one focus and spend political capital and budget. Shocks roll at real base rates. Reshuffles come when they come.
  3. Four real siblings that started at your income run alongside you. Some will take off. You will see which, and why.
  4. At the end the game reruns your career to show what your choices changed, what luck did, and which reforms outlived you.
A game by Jonathan Mazumdar, for people who work on growth. Data: World Bank WDI, Enterprise Surveys and Worldwide Governance Indicators. Every judgement call is on the methods page. No login; nothing leaves your browser.
